About Me!


Alex Doftoreanu is a recent Full Time Diploma program graduate at On The Mic Training who goes over the top in their voice work (unless they are asked to tone it down, of course). Alex loves expressing themselves through their voice, whether it be developing their characters for animation and video games, finding the best way to show off products during commercials, or hooking people in with their voice in narration. They are excited to dive into this industry with an open mind and get their voice out there!
